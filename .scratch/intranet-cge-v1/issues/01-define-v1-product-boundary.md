# Define the v1 product boundary

Type: grilling
Status: resolved
Blocked by:

## Question

What must v1 own, and what must remain outside this effort?

## Answer

V1 owns the application shell, local access control, people administration, the employee directory, privacy-aware birthday reminders, vacation request approvals, CSV provisioning, and audit visibility. It is a workflow layer rather than the authoritative payroll or personnel system. Other legacy-page capabilities are future modules.
