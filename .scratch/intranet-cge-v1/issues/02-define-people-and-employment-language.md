# Define people and employment language

Type: grilling
Status: resolved
Blocked by:

## Question

How should people, their relationship to CGE, organizational placement, and login access be distinguished?

## Answer

A Person represents a human. An Employment Relationship records that person's relationship to CGE, category, primary organizational unit, explicit supervisor, and history. A User Account grants application access and may be absent for directory-only workers. A person has at most one active employment relationship in v1. Employment categories decide vacation eligibility.
