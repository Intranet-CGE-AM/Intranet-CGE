# Product

## Register

product

## Users

CGE Amazonas employees, supervisors, HR staff, and platform administrators use
this intranet inside the government network. They need to find people, manage
employment data, request and decide vacation workflows, and administer access
without learning technical system terminology.

## Product Purpose

Provide one trustworthy internal platform for CGE operational modules. Success
means each person can identify the right module, understand the next action,
complete sensitive HR and access workflows safely, and verify what happened
without relying on informal support.

## Brand Personality

Institutional, calm, precise. The product should feel reliable and premium
without becoming ceremonial, decorative, or cold.

## Anti-references

- Generic AI-generated SaaS dashboards with repeated cards, colored icon tiles,
  inflated metrics, excessive rounding, gradients, or decorative motion.
- Financial-product metaphors and visual language that do not belong in a
  government intranet.
- Interfaces that expose database or policy jargon without explaining the
  decision a user must make.
- Dense administration screens that make unrelated workflows look equally
  important.

## Design Principles

1. Name the decision, not the implementation.
2. Show only the actions available to the current role and workflow state.
3. Keep module context visible while separating platform administration.
4. Prevent irreversible mistakes and explain the consequence before action.
5. Prefer direct, contextual guidance over manuals, tooltips, and support
   dependency.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Meet WCAG 2.2 AA and Brazilian eMAG expectations. Support keyboard-only use,
visible focus, semantic labels and errors, screen readers, reduced motion,
high-contrast states, 390 px mobile layouts, and clear PT-BR language for users
with different levels of technical familiarity.
